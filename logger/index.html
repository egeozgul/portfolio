Device calibration
Stream at the full sample rate, then work through the four steps. Everything is computed here from the raw data: nothing the board records changes. The result can be saved to the probe, where it is added to its calibration files (nothing is overwritten), and downloaded. Load the board's current config first, so its device ID and any steps you skip are kept.
the poses, rolled positions and magnetometer cloud, to recompute later
1 · Accelerometer bias and scale
Hold the tube completely still in 12 or more orientations, about 3 s each — every axis pointing roughly up once and down once, plus some in between. A pose is captured automatically after 2 s of stillness, if it is at least 20° from every earlier one.
2 · Magnetometer hard and soft iron
Uses the magnetometer cloud in the Field Calibration tab (Fit now works from here): tumble the tube through every direction until the coverage is high, then fit. Do it outdoors, away from metal, with the tube fully assembled as deployed and the battery above 3.6 V.
3 · Accelerometer alignment in the tube
Hold the tube in a fixed stand at any angle — upright, leaning, or lying in a V-block — and roll it about its own axis, roughly 30° each time, pausing about 3 s, for 8–12 positions over at least three-quarters of a turn. Keep the stand or V-block fixed: only the tube turns, and neither needs to be level or vertical. Rolling makes the measured "up" direction trace a circle whose axis is the tube's axis: the chip's tilt relative to that axis is the result, and the setup's own angle is excluded. Uses step 1's bias and scale. Upright, it also uses step 2's magnetometer fit to tell how far the tube has turned; in a V-block it doesn't need the magnetometer at all.
4 · Magnetometer alignment to the accelerometer
Uses step 1's still poses, which recorded both sensors. Finds the small rotation between the two chips that makes the dip the same in every pose. Do steps 1 and 2 first — and take step 1's poses outdoors too, or the dip will vary with the room, not the chips.

Validation · gyro, accelerometer, magnetometer
acc = from gravity alone. gyro = gyro integrated from the last reset, no correction, so it drifts — its drift rate is the gyro's bias. comb = complementary filter: gyro for fast motion, gravity pulling it back with time constant τ. Gravity is ignored while |a| is outside 1 g ± the gate, because the accelerometer is then measuring motion as well as gravity. Heading axis is the axis the device turns about — for a probe, its long axis. Tilt is measured from that axis to vertical, and heading uses the magnetometer's other two axes (for +x: My and Mz). Pick the sign the axis points when the probe hangs normally: the accelerometer should read +1 g along it. Heading assumes the magnetometer axes match the IMU axes — use the Mag axes field in the orientation panel if they don't.

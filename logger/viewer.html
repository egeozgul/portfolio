    Trajectory

    Field magnetometer calibration

    A tumble recorded on site gives the magnetometer’s hard and soft iron as they were that day. Only the iron is replaced: the accelerometer corrections, chip tilt and the rotation between the chips still come from the lab file. Calibration sessions are fitted as soon as they load. Each recording then uses the field calibration nearest to it in time, or the one you choose under its name.

    Stations

    Each pause while pulling is a station: its angles are averaged over the still part of the pause, so each one is a clean, separate reading. The continuous columns average the moving samples within the set distance of the same depth, from both the way down and the way up. The sentence above each table compares the stations with the merged continuous path.

    Summary

    Quality columns: with a good calibration, |G| stays at 1 g with a small spread, and the field strength and dip barely change however the tube is turned.

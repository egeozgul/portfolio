BoreLogger probe

Opens the probe’s own page: checks, recording, runs and firmware update.
  1. Plug the probe into this phone with its USB cable.
  2. Wait a few seconds, then tap Open probe page.
  3. For a firmware update, use the Update tab there. It needs the phone’s internet (4G or Wi-Fi).
Open probe page
Opening in 3 s · stay here
The probe page is http://10.10.10.10, served by the probe over the cable. If it does not open, the probe is not connected yet: check the cable and try again.
Probe page will not open? On a computer, or an Android phone with Chrome, the USB updater installs the firmware over the cable directly. Probes that do not have the Update tab yet need it once.
